# Gate 29 — Institutional Workflow Choice and Policy-Aware DT Handoffs

Version 1.0.0. Additive research implementation; all earlier gates remain unchanged.
Frozen before any comparative confirmation execution. Results are recorded separately.

## Terminology and evidence status

- **Reference decision specification:** the frozen modeled obligations, action contracts, authority conditions, and objective.
- **Reference outcomes:** all acceptable complete workflows, and the best permissible partial workflows when the task cannot yet be completed.
- **Independent reference evaluator:** a separately coded exhaustive finite evaluator used only for assessment. The same developer authored both implementations and the scenarios. This is implementation independence, not independent clinical/legal review.
- **Runtime policy validator:** the shared component that checks proposed actions and their joint effects immediately before modeled execution. Reference outcomes never authorize actions.

Source applicability and independent clinical/Korean legal review remain **HOLD / pending**. No new claim about law, clinical safety, model generalization, or production deployment is made. The original Gate 29 draft is expanded into a bounded engineering study, not silently treated as expert-approved policy.

## Thirteen required reporting elements

1. **Question.** Can institution-owned alternatives preserve a feasible clinical-review obligation and complete a jointly authorized task when the originally requested path fails? Can the receiving workspace preserve the associated context and reject changed authority before commitment?
2. **Frozen assumptions.** All methods receive exactly the same authorized metadata, actual candidate action contracts, available authority records, requirements, and lexicographic objective. Trusted modeled event publisher and authority register; no real hospital signatures or credentials. Clinical review is modeled, not diagnosis. Freshness is two event steps, a declared engineering parameter rather than a clinical threshold. Available evidence means candidate source availability, not legal approval.
3. **Changed component.** Workflow selection in the main comparison. Separate one-field DT-context ablations and change-before-commit probes. Same new Gate 29 runtime validator for every method. This is an additive research execution layer; the old gate validators and live UI are not replaced.
4. **Dataset/provenance.** Eighteen developer-authored scenario-modeling families. Actions originate as frozen institution-owned proposal contracts, not from an LLM or a newly trained model. They contain field names and authority metadata, not real patient records. Tasks cover clinical–cyber handoff, purpose-specific research views, and local DT exchange. Snapshot selection is evaluated at a specified released-event state; this is not a new end-to-end progressive interface evaluation.
5. **Split.** Four development families and fourteen predeclared internal confirmation families. Initial 6/12 authorship was conservatively regrouped to 4/14 BEFORE any outcome evaluation: D02 and D03 were moved into confirmation because their dependency skeletons matched confirmation cases. Original IDs were retained, so a D-prefix does not determine the final split. Use the explicit `split` field. No confirmation family was used for development tests. Shared rules and action primitives remain; no external or unseen-law generalization claim.
6. **Leakage audit.** Conservative dependency-graph fingerprints keep matching skeletons together; exact record overlap and cross-split group overlap are checked. Fingerprinting is not proof of complete semantic independence. Family IDs, split/track/motif labels, operator-private views, and event histories are stripped before selection. No feasibility labels or reference outcomes enter the selectors. The trusted current authority register is an input, not a hidden answer. The new module does not load saved classifiers or the multilingual retrieval backend.
7. **Reference evaluation.** Separate exhaustive subset enumeration, local constraint checks, and dependency-order witness construction. All valid sets are retained; any equally valid alternative is accepted. Four hand-specified development fixtures check disposition and best objective. Separate assessment checks actual committed order, not just a preferred action set. Same-developer implementations can share specification mistakes; external review is pending.
8. **Metrics.** Feasible-workflow recall among reference-feasible confirmation families; reference-optimal full/partial workflow count; disposition correctness; clinical completion when independently feasible; actual commit constraint violations; selected alternative use; audit integrity. Selection-only operation counts and single-run durations are descriptive, not an efficiency superiority claim. Report N/A for zero denominators. Adapter and temporal perturbations are correlated probes, not extra independent cases. No measured peer-message privacy advantage is claimed.
9. **Pass/fail.** Engineering integrity requires all development tests, frozen dependencies, zero observed invalid commits, and intact receipt chains. Reference agreement and comparative superiority are SEPARATE statuses. There is no requirement that MedVerse beat the fair selector or complete planner. Clinical/legal review remains pending regardless of numeric results. Any failed confirmation is retained; no threshold or method changes based on that failure are authorized in this release.
10. **Raw outputs.** Per-family metrics, reference feasible sets and witness orders, selected proposal IDs, actual proposals, precommit verdicts, institution-owned modeled effects, handoff records, source-record hashes, source/authority snapshots, paired comparisons, context-ablation rows, temporal probes, environment, checks, and presentation manifests. New runs receive unique timestamped directories.
11. **Interpretation.** An advantage over the requested-path-only control establishes the effect of considering alternatives, not general multi-agent superiority. A tie with capable exact selectors remains a tie. Distinguish reference-specification ambiguity, missing candidate coverage, representation error, algorithm error, and environmental failure. No learning capacity or optimization claim can follow from this deterministic study.
12. **Claim boundary.** Finite modeled workflow choice, local receipt auditing, and two local workspace adapters. No clinical treatment, real disclosure, real regulatory approval, legal compliance certification, production security, or complete ITU Recommendation conformance. The policy KB contains real-source-derived candidate records; the action permissions and clinical obligations are modeling assumptions pending review.
13. **Next dependency.** Qualified review of the scenario contracts and reference outcomes; independent confirmation authorship; then a separately versioned integration with the live progressive UI. Preserve Gates 26–28 and all of their original evidence. Any new failure-driven repair needs its own version, justification, one-component change, and fresh confirmation.

## Comparator contracts

- **REQUEST_ONLY:** original-request scope control. Performs fair stateful search on the requested path, but does not adopt the explicitly marked alternatives. It receives the same candidate library. This is intentionally a limited scope control, NOT the strongest novelty comparator and NOT a substitute for B4.
- **FAIR_STATEFUL_SELECTOR:** exhaustive reachable-state search over all candidates, revisiting decisions. No arbitrary cutoff weakens it.
- **INDEPENDENT_CONSTRAINT_PLANNER:** separately implemented subset-constraint selection and valid-order construction; retains institutional action ownership.
- **MEDVERSE_HARMONIZER:** goal-directed assembly of institution-owned alternatives and prerequisite closure, followed by shared execution validation.
- **Reference evaluator:** assessment only, exhaustive independently coded subset validation; unavailable to every selector.

The common diagnostic feasibility classifier is held fixed and not credited as a separate harmonization gain. It distinguishes an available alternative that a selector did not choose from true insufficiency or pending authority.

## Feasibility and the frozen objective

All mandate, scope, authority, evidence, dependency, exclusivity, and joint-disclosure conditions are hard execution constraints. A human-review request does not create an approval. For permissible candidate sets the lexicographic objective is:

1. preserve the modeled clinical-review obligation whenever it is achievable;
2. satisfy as many required joint obligations as possible;
3. minimize distinct disclosed field–recipient pairs;
4. minimize action count.

Clinical continuity is not traded for a lower disclosure cost. If all goals are feasible, the first two components are zero. If not, safe partial work can continue while the system separately returns pending human authority, insufficient evidence, or no feasible workflow under the modeled conditions. Joint field restrictions are explicit task specifications, not inferred Korean statutory prohibitions.

## Clinical–cyber handoff

Record the affected action and institution, prerequisites, established obligations, requested clinical review, and receipt hash. Cyber changes technical state but cannot issue a Hospital action. The coordinator cannot invent a trusted route. The present selector receives authorized contract metadata; no raw peer clinical data is needed.

## Purpose-bound workspace requests

Research alternatives preserve the declared task purpose and must satisfy task adequacy and their own grants. A limited view is not automatically lawful because it is smaller. Authority records are trusted modeled inputs, not user assertions. When purposes, epochs, or policy bindings change before commitment, the actual selected action is rechecked.

## DT exchange context

Two local adapters serialize the same envelope directly or map its keys into another workspace representation. The receiver checks required fields, provenance, validity, observed-event freshness, recipient, purpose, information scope, policy snapshot, epoch, expiry, and membership in the trusted modeled source registry. Missing metadata never upgrades permission. Schema/hash checks rely on the trusted local publisher; they do not provide cryptographic remote authenticity. A second independently coded receiver checks the same constraints.

One-field omission/corruption probes are ablations, not representatives of an ITU-compliant competing platform. The international source mapping below is inherited comparison context from the supplied draft, not a new standards or legal gap finding.

## Source-to-investigation mapping and limitations

- T1: Medical Service Act / PIPA candidate security records and modeled clinical-continuity obligations; WHO health-AI principles motivate the responsibility question. No clinical threshold is derived from those records.

- T3: Medical record integrity candidate record and explicit modeled receiving-side checks. Y.4239/Y.4240 are comparison architecture/interface anchors; the study does not claim that these standards lack access control or metadata.

The exact copied candidate records and their hashes are in `data/coordination29/source_snapshot.json`. Dates and interpretations were not freshly legally verified. Source availability and hash consistency are not applicability validation.
