# Evaluation organized by research question

> Final cropped figures and their report captions are available in the [Results Gallery](RESULTS_GALLERY.md).

## RQ1 — Evidence-grounded institutional decisions

Evidence: audited Privacy/Cyber components; Digital Twin provenance/freshness; Korean/English policy retrieval.

## RQ2 — Architectural governance

Frozen B1–B5 comparison: Single Omniscient, Unconstrained MAS, Consensus/Debate reference, Policy Filter Only, MedVerse-Agent. Main metrics: Institutional Correctness (IC), Coordination Quality (CQ), Exact Workflow Match (EWM), Policy Violation Rate (PVR), Unnecessary Escalation Rate (UER), Information Overexposure Rate (IOR), Unsafe Execution Rate (UXR).

Key B4→B5 observation on the controlled benchmark: CQ 0.600→1.000 and EWM 0.143→1.000.

## RQ3 — Dynamic coordination under partial information

Substudies isolate proposal-order robustness, authorized-alternative selection, Digital Twin context handoff and bounded commitment timing. Capable comparator ties are retained. Bounded waiting improves some continuations and loses others; it is not presented as universally superior.

Historical gate identifiers remain in the repository only for reproducibility and raw evidence lookup.
