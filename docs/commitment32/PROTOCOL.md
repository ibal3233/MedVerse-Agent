# Gate 32 — bounded commitment control: experiment v1.0

Status at authorship: specification and development implementation; confirmation not yet run. The release freeze records the final code/data hashes before confirmation execution. The earlier design-only specification is preserved separately.

## Terminology
Use **reference decision specification**, **reference outcomes**, **reference evaluator**, and **runtime policy validator**. The full-information reference is a noncausal assessment upper bound, never a runtime comparator. Separately coded assessment is not independent external clinical/legal approval.

## Thirteen reporting elements

1. **Question.** Does a visible, bounded institutional review condition justify delaying an otherwise currently authorized irreversible action, while preserving immediate obligations and useful partial work? What does waiting cost when an alternative is late, denied, or absent?
2. **Frozen assumptions.** A trusted local publisher releases one modeled event at a time. Authority records, source availability, tasks, review scope, responsible role and expiration are declared inputs, not patient data or clinical guidelines. Time is event steps, not seconds or a clinical urgency limit. Immediate obligations and latest commitment times are visible. Waiting is not permission. No action can be undone retrospectively. Same action/authority sets and common non-disclosing preparations are available to all methods.
3. **Changed component.** Commitment timing only in the primary comparison. The existing `coordination29.selectors.select` (MEDVERSE_HARMONIZER configuration) and `coordination29.validator` remain byte-identical. A shared deadline admission check and preparatory-action handler are explicitly added to the new stream runtime for ALL comparators. The preparation vocabulary uses the existing institutional action kinds; preparation-only effects are distinct from care, disclosure and authority. It is not a reversal of actual execution. New contract metadata are experimental inputs, not added hints in C01/C02.
4. **Dataset/provenance.** Twelve internally authored information-prefix families: four development families (two branches each), eight confirmation families (four branches each). Forty continuation branches, three causal controllers, six events per branch. Medical scenarios model institutions and action dependencies, not treatment. Sources are inherited real-source-derived candidate records; this study does not newly verify legal applicability. No LLM/classifier inference or embedding retrieval is performed.
5. **Split.** Family-grouped 4/8 development/confirmation. Every continuation of a prefix is kept in the same split. Development fixtures establish basic mechanism behavior only. Confirmation adds multi-institution dependencies, context, revocation, and future-goal structure. All datasets/methods are fixed before confirmation; no comparative outcome targets are required for integrity PASS.
6. **Leakage audit.** The online Session receives only the current event, not a complete family, branch label, future sequence, reference answer, or horizon-complete flag. Strict input fields reject extra future data. Exact prefix/structure fingerprint overlap is checked across splits. Pairwise equal-history comparisons must yield identical policy inputs and decisions. Shared primitives and developer authorship remain; fingerprints are not proof of general semantic independence.
7. **Reference outcomes.** New commits are checked by a separate constraint implementation, with past valid effects retained after later revocation. An exhaustive finite timeline enumeration supplies an offline full-information completion bound. It can wait with hindsight and is labelled noncausal. Current authority, prerequisites, modeled timing and joint disclosure constraints are enforced. No reference information reaches controllers. Clinical priority and on-time goal completion are assessed separately from disclosure cost and step counts.
8. **Metrics.** Completion within declared limits (all branches and offline-feasible subset); clinical completion when the reference can achieve it; proposal-wait steps (NOT clinical waiting time); invalid commits; unjustified waits; permitted preparation counts; per-institution decision opportunities and commits; receipt integrity; same-history consistency; paired gains and losses versus eager; bounded-control ties. Correlated branches are not independent patients. Single-run durations are descriptive, with no speed-superiority claim.
9. **Pass/fail.** Engineering integrity requires development checks, frozen-file integrity, zero observed invalid commits and unjustified waits, same-history consistency, change-before-commit rejection, and unchanged historical event outcomes. Comparative gains and losses are separate. Any waiting loss, late alternative, original-case failure or fair-control tie is retained. No post-confirmation threshold, label, scope, action, or controller changes are authorized.
10. **Raw outputs.** Actual event payloads, visible-input hashes, timing decisions with reason/owner/start/expiry, preparations, real proposal IDs, final verdicts, source-record hashes, append-only effects, receipt chains, per-branch CSV/JSON, reference witnesses, paired comparisons, per-role counts, causal checks, mutation probes, environment, frozen manifests, and figure provenance.
11. **Interpretation.** A gain on explicit review-contract cases is not a repair of historical cases with no such information. A competent bounded-wait controller may match MedVerse. Waiting can lose a genuinely authorized opportunity before revocation; that earlier execution is not retroactively illegal. A causal method cannot promise the hindsight optimum on indistinguishable prefixes. No clinical-delay claim follows from event steps.
12. **Claim boundary.** Finite deterministic commitment engineering, trusted local modeled authority and event publisher, no real patients/disclosure/treatment, no cryptographic remote credentials, no deployment safety or legal certification. This is a new research runtime, not an automatic replacement of the final medical interface.
13. **Next dependency.** Qualified contract/source review, fresh external case authorship, then a separately versioned final clinical-interface/video integration. Known C01/C02 and all earlier gates are retained. A repair after confirmation would require a new documented intervention and fresh confirmation.

## Methods

- **EAGER:** unchanged local authorization and goal-directed selection; immediately commit selected permitted actions.
- **FAIR_BOUNDED_WAIT:** independently written capable policy using the same visible review conditions, expiry, obligations, prepared operations, selector and validator. No artificial search cutoff or weakened information.
- **MEDVERSE_COMMITMENT:** explicit per-proposal decision records containing owner, history hash, prerequisites, review reference, wait start/expiry, available preparations and final revalidation flag.
- **FULL_INFORMATION_REFERENCE:** assessment only. Exhaustive hindsight-aware timeline search; never supplied to any controller.

Both bounded policies stop waiting at the earliest declared expiry/commit limit, cannot extend a remembered wait silently, and bypass optional waits for immediate commitments. No active pending review means no conjectured favorable future. Invalid authority/evidence yields BLOCK or ESCALATE, not a WAIT-derived authorization.

## Prefix-family coverage

Development: basic authorized care; bounded-expiry wait; immediate duty; pending authority.
Confirmation P01: clinical route/containment conflict; P02: containment breadth; P03: joint-purpose disclosure; P04: valid opportunity followed by authority change; P05: urgent independent duties; P06: DT recipient/provenance/version checks; P07: oversight and denied preparation; P08: unannounced future need.

All review times and deadlines are declared assumptions. No new Korean statutory obligation or clinical threshold is inferred from a source citation.

## Presentation and release

`medverse/gates/gate32_commitment_control.py` verifies, runs, saves, exports PNG/PDF/table, and displays by default. `--no-show`, `--no-plots`, and `--plot-only --run RUN_ID` are optional. No auto-refreeze command is exposed by the runner. Figures must show fair ties, adverse branches, and the noncausal status of the full-information reference. All output directories are unique; earlier runs are never overwritten.
