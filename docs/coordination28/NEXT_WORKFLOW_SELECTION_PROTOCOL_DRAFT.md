# Next dependency — Genuine workflow-choice harmonization

**Status: specification draft; not implemented, not executed, not a frozen claim.**

Gate 28 isolates scheduling. A strict advantage over a capable stateful scheduler is not established. Do not add more cosmetic variants or weaken that comparator. The next scientific object is choosing between institution-owned alternatives, not sorting one predefined release workflow.

1. **Question.** Can the system find an authorized joint workflow when the original request is infeasible but one or more institution-proposed alternatives are feasible, while preserving mandatory clinical review and information boundaries?
2. **Frozen assumptions to establish.** Same Korean source snapshot, validated mandate descriptors, candidate actions, evidence availability, authority requirements and objective for every comparator. No automatic permission from emergency context; no coordinator-created consent or approval. Legal applicability is a separately reviewed prerequisite, not an optimization output.
3. **Changed component.** A candidate-workflow selection module, separate from authorization. Agents propose alternatives within their own mandates. The coordinator selects; the unchanged execution gate disposes. Any changed purpose, data scope or recipient requires explicit revalidation and any newly required review.
4. **Dataset/provenance.** Independently authored modeling families must cover authorized restricted-view alternatives, incompatible purposes, authority-limited clinical/cyber actions, unavailable evidence, temporal approval changes and genuinely infeasible workflows. Do not derive oracle alternatives from the candidate planner. No patient observations are assumed.
5. **Split.** Group by constraint/alternative structure, not record ID or paraphrase. Reserve whole motifs for confirmation. Fix all choice rules and budget on development data. The number and assignment of families must be frozen before results; no count is claimed here.
6. **Leakage audit.** Separate accessible observations, proposed action descriptions and reference feasible-set labels. Do not encode the correct answer in a convenience feature. Keep paraphrases and isomorphic workflows in the same group. Audit policy templates, graph structure and generator overlap explicitly.
7. **Oracle.** A separately implemented bounded enumerator over the same allowed action alternatives, with independently reviewed constraints. It yields all valid workflows or an explicit no-feasible-workflow result. Manual review covers representative edge cases. Exhaustive enumeration validates finite specification compliance, not legal or clinical truth.
8. **Metrics.** Feasible-workflow recall; unauthorized-commit count; exact obligation-set satisfaction; legitimate clinical-continuation rate; unnecessary escalation; supported evidence requests; disclosure scope; valid alternative selection; objective gap relative to the finite oracle; messages, rounds and measured processing cost. Do not penalize a different but equally valid workflow.
9. **Pass/fail.** Integrity and constraint soundness are separate from superiority. Evaluate a static filter, a fair stateful selector, an independently implemented constraint planner, and the proposed harmonizer with equal inputs and budgets. A centralized comparator may retain institution-owned execution; do not force it to impersonate all roles. A tie with the complete planner is valid, not a failed experiment.
10. **Raw outputs.** Candidate actions and owners, provided observations, applicable obligations, every selected/rejected alternative, evidence queries, selected plan, final validations, side effects, oracle feasible sets, messages and timing. Record all failed cases.
11. **Interpretation.** Be explicit whether a gain comes from alternative enumeration, constraint solving, information routing, temporal handling or coordination protocol. These are different mechanisms. Do not attribute a conventional planner's capabilities to multi-agent novelty merely by renaming it.
12. **Claim boundary.** Finite modeled policy-consistency testing with candidate interpretations. No deployment, clinical outcome, unrestricted legal advice, new-law generalization or proof that no other planner can do the same thing.
13. **Next dependency.** Independent policy/clinical assessment of the candidate alternatives and oracle. Implementation begins only after the actionable constraints and comparator interfaces are specified. Freeze and then use fresh confirmation. Gate 26–28 results remain unchanged.

### Concrete mechanism example (proposed, not evaluated)

A Research agent requests identifiable patient-level monitoring data. Privacy does not authorize that request, but indicates the conditions for a permitted scoped view. The Research institution, not the coordinator, proposes an alternative purpose-preserving limited request. Required reviews are represented by genuine modeled authority records. The coordinator may assemble that alternate workflow without blocking independently authorized clinical review. The execution validator checks the alternative as a new action with its own evidence and approvals. If no valid basis exists, evidence request or human authority remains the correct outcome.

This is a protocol-design example. It is not a claim that any particular Korean statute permits the proposed disclosure, nor a claim that the current sandbox already performs such workflow synthesis.
