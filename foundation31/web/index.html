What this release establishes

Gate 31 adds an observation-aware bridge to the original Digital Twin and an explicit pinned semantic retrieval path. The unmodified Gate 29 selector is connected to progressively released events, institution-owned modeled action contracts, final revalidation and inspectable decision receipts.

Implemented and testable

Role-specific projections; alternative workflow selection; purpose and scope checks; DT context checks; authority changes; replay and duplicate handling; linked decision receipts; source-linked Korean/English retrieval with backend identity. Dense performance is not claimed until the pinned model is executed.

ITU functional mapping

SRC: Korean sources and modeled events. C: controlled intake. PP: source and access metadata. M: retrieval and bounded proposal contracts. P: validation. D: coordinated outcomes. SINK: modeled workspace actions.

Not established

Independent clinical or Korean legal review; hospital integration; real signatures; production identity isolation; live LLM inference; full ITU conformance; algorithmic superiority over capable exact planners.

Interpretation

All earlier gates remain frozen. Progressive integration is regression on known scenario models, not fresh independent confirmation. The medical-metaverse workspace is a local demonstration, not clinical deployment.
